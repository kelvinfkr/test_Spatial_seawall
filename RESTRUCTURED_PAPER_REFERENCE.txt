💡 KEY INNOVATION:

The central insight is that coastal seawall optimization (a priori 
infinite-dimensional) becomes "pairwise decomposable" once discretized, 
because:

1. Construction cost is strictly convex: C_i(h_i) is convex
2. Damage is strictly convex: E[(S_i - h_i)_+] is convex and decreasing
3. Spatial correlation decays exponentially: χ(d) ∝ e^{-λd}
4. These three facts together imply the discretized objective has
   a strictly tridiagonal Hessian (Theorem 3)
5. Tridiagonal structure means one-dimensional "chain" topology
6. Chain topology means pairwise KKT conditions imply global KKT
   (Theorem 1)

Result: Solve n-1 independent 2D problems → automatically global optimum
        for the n-dimensional problem.

This is not a heuristic approximation—it's mathematically guaranteed.
